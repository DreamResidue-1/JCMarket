JC Market - Privacy Policy (Demo)

This repository contains a demo privacy policy for the JC Market sample project. It is provided for demonstration purposes only and is not legal advice.

- Data collected: user-provided information (name, email), order history, and analytics events.
- Usage: data is used to fulfill orders, provide support, and improve the service.
- Retention: demo retention policy is 1 year for seed/demo data.
- Contact: privacy@jc-market.example
