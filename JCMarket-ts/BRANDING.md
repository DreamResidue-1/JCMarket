JC Market - Branding Guidelines

Brand essence
- Name: JC Market
- Tagline: Real Goods, Real People
- Tone: Trustworthy, friendly, modern

Colors
- Primary: #0B6EFD (blue)
- Accent: #00C2A8 (teal)
- Neutral: #0F1724 (dark slate), #F8FAFC (light)

Logo
- Use JCMarket-ts/logo.svg for web and marketing.
- Use favicon.svg as site favicon.

Typography
- Headings: Inter or Segoe UI
- Body: Roboto or system UI

Assets
- Place product placeholder SVGs in JCMarket-ts/ for demos.

Legal
- Keep privacy and terms drafts in JCMarket-ts/privacy.md and JCMarket-ts/terms.md
